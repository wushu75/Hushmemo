Why people choose it

Most note-takers send a bot into your call and a copy of the conversation to their servers. HushMemo does neither.

No bot in the call

Nothing joins the meeting and no one gets a “recording started” nudge about you. HushMemo listens from your side, from the browser tab.

Nothing is uploaded

Transcription runs on your computer. The audio never reaches our servers, because there are no servers — there is nothing to leak.

The notes are yours

Draft with a local model, or bring your own API key. Export any meeting as JSON, or delete it and it is gone for good.

Free and open

Unlimited meetings, no account to make, MIT-licensed. Read every line of the code and change whatever you like.

How it works

Three steps, from joining a call to a written record you can keep.

1

Start listening

Open the side panel on a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and press Start. A quiet teal dot shows it is on, and the call keeps playing as normal.

2

Jot as you go

Type rough fragments while you talk. Each one is timestamped against the transcript, so the context around it is never lost.

3

Get real notes

When you stop, HushMemo turns the transcript and your jottings into a summary with decisions, action items and open questions.

By default, HushMemo makes no network requests at all. Transcription and note-drafting run on your machine.

Connect a local model like Ollama, or paste your own cloud API key, and only then does anything leave your computer — and you set that up yourself.

Bring it to your next meeting

It runs offline the moment it is loaded, with a built-in transcription stub and a no-model note generator, so you can try the whole flow right away.

When you want real speech-to-text, drop in Whisper. For richer notes, point it at a local Ollama model or your own API key. The README covers both, and neither is required to start.

Get it on GitHub
  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Focus a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and click the HushMemo icon.
