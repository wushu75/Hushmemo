Meeting notes that stay on your machine.

HushMemo listens to your call from the browser tab — no bot ever joins the meeting — and turns the transcript and your own quick notes into a clean summary. Everything is processed and stored on your own computer.

Why it's different

Most note-takers send a bot into your call and a copy of your conversation to their servers. HushMemo does neither.

No bot in the call

Nothing joins your meeting. HushMemo captures the tab's audio from your side, so no one sees a recording bot in the participant list.

Local by default

Audio, transcript and notes are processed and kept on your computer. No account to make, nothing uploaded.

Your notes, your files

Export any meeting as JSON, or delete it and it's gone. There is no server holding a second copy.

Open and readable

Plain JavaScript, no build step, MIT-licensed. Read every line and change whatever you like.

How it works

Three steps, from joining a call to a written record you can keep.

1

Start listening

Open the side panel on a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and press Start. A quiet teal dot shows it's on, and the call keeps playing as normal.

2

Jot as you go

Type short notes during the call. Each one is timestamped against the transcript, so the context around it isn't lost.

3

Get structured notes

When you stop, HushMemo turns the transcript and your jottings into a summary with decisions, action items and open questions.

By default, HushMemo makes no network requests at all. Transcription and note-drafting run on your machine.

If you choose to connect a local model like Ollama, or paste your own cloud API key, only then does anything leave your computer — and you set that up yourself.

Get started

It runs offline out of the box, with a built-in transcription stub and a no-model note generator, so you can try the whole flow the moment it's loaded.

When you want real speech-to-text, drop in Whisper (via transformers.js). For richer notes, point it at a local Ollama model or your own API key. The README walks through both, and neither is required to start.

  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder.
  4. Open a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and click the HushMemo icon.
