Seven reasons people are quietly switching

Most meeting note-takers send a bot into the call and a copy of the conversation to their servers. Here is why people are choosing one that does neither.

1

No bot ever joins the call

Nothing shows up in the participant list, and no one gets a “recording started” warning with your name on it. HushMemo listens from your side, straight from the browser tab.

2

Your audio never leaves the machine

Speech-to-text runs on your own computer. The meeting audio is never uploaded, so there is simply nothing to leak, subpoena, or lose.

3

It works in every meeting app

Meet, Zoom, Teams — HushMemo captures the browser tab’s audio, so it does not depend on any one platform’s integration or blessing.

4

Your own thoughts, pinned to the moment

Timestamped jottings attach your half-formed thoughts to the exact point in the transcript, so the context around them is never lost.

5

Real structure, not a wall of text

You get a summary, the decisions, action items with owners, and open questions — the shape of a note you can actually send.

6

No account, no subscription, no lock-in

Nothing to sign up for. Export any meeting as JSON, or delete it and it is gone for good. The data is yours the whole way through.

7

Open source you can actually read

Plain JavaScript, no build step, MIT-licensed. Audit the privacy claims yourself, or fork it and make it your own.

How it works

Three steps, from joining a call to a written record you can keep.

1

Start listening

Open the side panel on a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and press Start. A quiet teal dot shows it is on, and the call keeps playing as normal.

2

Jot as you go

Type rough fragments while you talk. Each one is timestamped against the transcript, so the context around it is never lost.

3

Get real notes

When you stop, HushMemo turns the transcript and your jottings into a summary with decisions, action items and open questions.

By default, HushMemo makes no network requests at all. Transcription and note-drafting run on your machine.

Connect a local model like Ollama, or paste your own cloud API key, and only then does anything leave your computer — and you set that up yourself.

Bring it to your next meeting

It runs offline the moment it is loaded, with a built-in transcription stub and a no-model note generator, so you can try the whole flow right away.

When you want real speech-to-text, drop in Whisper. For richer notes, point it at a local Ollama model or your own API key. The README covers both, and neither is required to start.

Get it on GitHub
  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Focus a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab and click the HushMemo icon.
